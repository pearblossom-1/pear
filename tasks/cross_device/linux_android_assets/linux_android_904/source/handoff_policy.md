# Audio transcript handoff policy

A case is eligible for handoff only when:

1. its case ID, approval code, and owner match the request on the phone;
2. the corresponding register row has status `approved`; and
3. the coordinator contact uses the phone number from that approved row.

Pending and hold rows are not authorized for handoff, even when they share an
owner with the approved request.

The handoff document and short note must identify the selected case, owner,
approval code, source register, and an affirmative ready decision.
